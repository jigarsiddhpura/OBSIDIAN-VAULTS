
Here's the full cycle, using Anant Raj's own 2026 chart as the live example:

|Phase|What's happening|Price/volume signature|ARL example|
|---|---|---|---|
|**1. Accumulation**|Smart money quietly buys after a decline, while price moves sideways in a range. Public sentiment is still bearish/apathetic.|Range-bound, low volatility, volume often flat-to-rising on up-days within the range|The ₹403-431 zone in March 2026 where RSI hit ~26 (oversold) — this is where a base _would_ form if one is forming|
|**2. Markup**|Price breaks out of the accumulation range and trends up. This is the "recovery" you're thinking of — it's really just Phase 2.|Higher highs, higher lows, price above rising 50/200-DMA, volume expanding on up-moves|ARL's move from ~₹430 → ~₹583-610 through Q2/Q3 2026 — currently here|
|**3. Distribution**|After a strong run, smart money starts selling into strength while price goes sideways near highs. Public sentiment is now euphoric/bullish.|Range-bound near highs, volume often high but price stops making new highs, momentum indicators (RSI/MACD) start diverging from price|Not yet visible for ARL — would show up if price stalls somewhere near the ₹700-743 zone|
|**4. Markdown**|Distribution completes, breakout to the downside, trend reverses down.|Lower highs, lower lows, price below moving averages, volume expands on down-moves|This is what happened _before_ March 2026 — the drop from the 52-week high (₹743.65) down to ₹403|

So the cycle is: **Accumulation → Markup → Distribution → Markdown → (back to Accumulation)**, repeating.

Where ARL sits right now: it looks like it's in **Phase 2 (Markup)**, having left the March accumulation zone, but it hasn't yet approached its 52-week high closely enough to tell whether Phase 3 (Distribution) is starting.
