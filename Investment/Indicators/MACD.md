
### MACD — trend confirmation, slightly different angle from RSI

(MACD = **Moving Average Convergence Divergence**)

**What it measures:** The difference between two moving averages (typically 12-day and 26-day [[EMA]]), plus a signal line (9-day [[EMA]] of that difference). The "histogram" is the gap between MACD and its signal line.

- **Fast Length = 12** (this is the 12-day EMA)
- **Slow Length = 26** (this is the 26-day EMA)
- **Signal Smoothing = 9** (this is the 9-day EMA of the MACD line itself)

> **Why it's used alongside [[RSI]], not instead of it:** RSI tells you about momentum _intensity_; MACD tells you about trend _direction and turning points_. They can disagree, and when they do, it's informative.

**How to read it for your question:**

- **Histogram expanding (bars growing taller above zero):** Trend is accelerating — the breakout has legs.
- **Histogram shrinking even though price is still rising:** Classic early-warning sign — price is going up but the underlying trend strength is fading. This is exactly the setup where RSI can be high but the move is about to run out of steam. If you see this, temper your ₹743 expectation.
- **MACD line crossing above signal line right around the breakout:** Adds confirmation. If it crossed a few days _before_ the ₹600 breakout, that's a good lead indicator that this move was building, not sudden.

**How to read what shows up:**

- **MACD line (usually blue)** = the difference between the 12 EMA and 26 EMA.
- **Signal line (usually orange)** = 9-day EMA of that MACD line — basically a smoothed version, same concept as the yellow RSI-MA line you just saw.
- **Histogram (bars)** = the gap between MACD line and Signal line, shown visually — bars grow taller when the gap widens, shrink when it narrows.

**Simple beginner rule (same crossover logic as RSI):**

- **MACD line crosses above Signal line** → bullish, trend confirming upward momentum.
- **MACD line crosses below Signal line** → bearish, trend confirming downward momentum.
- **Histogram flipping from red to green (or shrinking red bars)** → early hint that a bullish crossover may be coming soon.
